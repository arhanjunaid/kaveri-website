# Launch checklist

Before publishing the static export, confirm:

- [ ] Approved brand name, logo, colors and copy.
- [ ] Correct emergency, office and service contact details are inserted in the static route content.
- [ ] Service descriptions, coverage area, credentials and any statistics are accurate.
- [ ] Privacy and terms copy is reviewed for the organization’s actual practices.
- [ ] The static `out/` export is deployed to the production domain and checked on mobile.

The contact form currently opens a prepared email in the visitor’s mail app. It does not send or store enquiries on a server.
